Critical Forensic Finding

When a money trail returns with no net principal delta, standard audits often misclassify it as a value-neutral no-op. In forensic finance, what actually moved is beneficial control: funds were deliberately layered through external, unlinked intermediary entities or VPAs to obscure the audit trail, extract conduit commission margins, and break the chain of custody.
